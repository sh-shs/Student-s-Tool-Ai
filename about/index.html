Our Background

Our Vision & Story

Why Student's Tool & AI Was Created

As students, we experienced the daily frustration of jumping between clunky calculator websites covered in ads, unverified formulas, and fragmented tools. Simple tasks like calculating required attendance percentages or estimating term GPAs shouldn't require navigating complex interfaces.

We created Student's Tool & AI to solve this problem once and for all. By bringing together over 30 academic tools and an intelligent AI study companion into one modern web portal, we give students a reliable home base for their academic journey.

🚀 Looking Ahead

Our roadmap includes multilingual options (including full Bengali support), advanced study analytics, offline capabilities, and expanded AI study features to support students globally.
