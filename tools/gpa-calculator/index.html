How It Works

Your Semester GPA is calculated by multiplying the numerical point value of each letter grade by the course credit hours to get total grade points, then dividing the sum of grade points by total credit hours attempted.

Standard 4.0 Scale Grade Values: A (4.0), A- (3.7), B+ (3.3), B (3.0), B- (2.7), C+ (2.3), C (2.0), C- (1.7), D+ (1.3), D (1.0), F (0.0).
